# Application of Group Theory to Music Theory

This repository accompanies the research work:

**Application of Group Theory to Music Theory**  
MSc Thesis, University of Lagos  
Author: Samuel Oluniyi Olusola  
Supervisor: Prof. J. O. Olaleru  

The work develops a mathematical framework for understanding musical
structures using group theory, symmetry, and algebraic transformations.

## Abstract
Music exhibits rich internal structure governed by symmetry and
transformational rules. This work applies concepts from group theory,
including group actions, cyclic groups, and representations, to model
musical scales, intervals, and harmonic transformations.

The approach provides a rigorous mathematical interpretation of
structural equivalence and invariance in music theory.

## Mathematical Themes
- Group actions on pitch-class sets
- Cyclic and dihedral group structures
- Transformations and symmetry in musical scales
- Algebraic invariants of musical objects

## Research Status
- Original MSc thesis (Grade A)
- Extended into an independent research manuscript
- Preprint available on **arXiv (math.GR, 2026)**

## Relevance Beyond Music
The symmetry-based framework developed here connects naturally to:
- Graph symmetry and automorphism groups
- Invariance and equivariance in learning systems
- Structural modeling in applied mathematics and AI theory
